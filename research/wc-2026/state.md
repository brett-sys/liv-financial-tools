## Per-stage advancement & market rules

### Group stage (COMPLETE)
- 12 groups of 4, 3 games each (MD1, MD2, MD3)
- Top 2 in each group + 8 best third-place teams advance to R32
- **Rotation-risk callouts applied on MD3** — teams already clinched
  their spot could rest starters. This was a real edge in group stage
  and is why "team X only needs a draw" reads had value in that window.
- MD3 also had "must-win" scenarios where teams push for goals, opposite
  incentive

### Round of 32 (CURRENT)
- Single elimination
- **No draws** — game goes to Extra Time and PKs if tied at 90'
- **ML settles at 90' + added time.** Extra time and PKs do NOT change
  the ML market. Draw ML wins if the score is level after regulation.
- **Both teams play to win** — no rotation, no parking, no sleepwalking
  narratives. Any read that assumes "team X may rest" is wrong here.

### Round of 16, QF, SF, F
- Same knockout rules as R32
- Increasing squad fatigue by SF/F stages — may matter for totals
  by the deep rounds, not yet

## Rotation-risk callouts (KO stage)

**NONE.** In knockout rounds no team can afford to rotate — one loss and
you're out. Do not carry "team X may rest starters" reasoning into any
R32-or-later read. That narrative is DEAD until we're back to a
tournament with a group stage.

The only KO-stage roster context that matters:
- **Injuries / suspensions** (real, verifiable, ideally cited by beat reporter)
- **Yellow-card accumulation** (a starter one booking away from a KO stage
  suspension may play carefully — real effect, not "sleepwalking")
- **Late-game keeper decisions** — some managers prefer a "PK specialist"
  sub — pre-90 goalie change usually not tactical
